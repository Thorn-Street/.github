# Thorn Street Company LLC

**Geospatial data infrastructure for the quiet businesses of the world.**

---

## About

Thorn Street is a geospatial engineering firm specializing in spatial data infrastructure, spatial databases, and production GIS applications. The company designs and builds systems for organizations that depend on location data, including data sourcing, pipelines, database architecture, spatial analysis, routing, and custom software applications.

Engineering-first, platform-agnostic, and focused on bringing rigorous spatial systems to underserved operational businesses.

## What we do

- **Data Sourcing & Acquisition** — identifying the right source for the question, vendor coordination, validation, and QA
- **Pipelines & Infrastructure** — spatial database design, ETL pipelines, GIS integrations, automated QA/QC
- **Analysis & Intelligence** — spatial analysis, risk and portfolio modeling, natural language queries over structured data
- **Maps, Dashboards & Delivery** — interactive web maps, dashboards, APIs, and clean dataset handoffs
